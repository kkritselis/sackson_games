Le Truc

How to play. French bluffing with a 32-card deck. Rank beats suit: 7 is highest, then 8, Ace, King, Queen, Jack, 10, 9. Win two of three tricks (a same-rank pair spoils a trick). Before playing you may raise the stake; refuse a raise and your opponent scores the current stake. First to 12 points wins a game; best of three games wins the rubber.
