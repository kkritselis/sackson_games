Last Word

How to play. A 9×9 grid starts with 9 random center letters. Place a letter adjacent (including diagonally) to at least two existing letters. Using the new letter plus letters in line with it (anagrams allowed along each line), form words in at least two directions. Score is the product of the lengths of those words. Any alphabetic string of length ≥ 2 is accepted for playability. Game ends when all four edges have at least one letter. Highest score wins. Vs computer.
