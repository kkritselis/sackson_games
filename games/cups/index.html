Cups

How to play. Each side has four cups and a pot, sowing toward your pot. On your turn either place 1–4 beans from stock into consecutive cups from the far left, or sow a cup that holds exactly enough beans to reach the pot. Placing so your last bean lands in an empty cup captures the opposite cup into your pot. Most beans in the pot wins.
