Lines of Action

How to play. Black (you) starts on the top and bottom edges; White (computer) on the left and right. Move a piece exactly as many squares as there are pieces on that line (orthogonally or diagonally). You may jump your own pieces but not enemies; landing on an enemy captures it. Connect all your remaining pieces into one group (orthogonal or diagonal contact) to win.
