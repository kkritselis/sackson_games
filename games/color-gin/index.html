Color Gin

How to play. Gin Rummy rules, but you keep four suit games at once. When you knock or gin, deadwood difference (plus bonuses) scores only in suits that are “clean” in your hand: a sequence spoils its suit; unmatched cards spoil their suits; a three-of-a-kind spoils the missing suit. You may knock only if at least one suit scores. First to 100 in a suit closes that column. Vs computer.
