Cutting Corners

How to play. A square has your color on two adjacent sides (blue) and the computer’s on the other two (orange). Players take turns drawing an L-shaped path (one right-angle turn) from edge to edge. Turn 1 needs 0 crossings, then 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5 crossings. Each new line must cross an opponent line or touch an opponent-colored edge. After six lines, regions are scored: more of your color on a region’s boundary wins it. Best of two rounds. Simplified: play uses a discrete 8×8 cell grid with axis-aligned L paths.

Click three points: start on an edge, corner of the L, then end on an edge.
