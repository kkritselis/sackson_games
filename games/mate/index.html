Mate

How to play. You and the computer each hold ten cards from a 20-card deck (A, 10, K, Q, 7 of each suit). Lead any card; the opponent must answer with the same suit, or if they have none, the same rank. The higher card (or higher suit when ranks match) leads the next move. Give mate by leading a card your opponent cannot follow — that card’s value times the move number is your score.
