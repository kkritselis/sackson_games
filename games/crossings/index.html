Crossings

How to play. White (you) vs Black (AI). Move a single piece one step in any direction, or surge a straight phalanx up to as many squares as pieces in the group. Capture a smaller enemy group or lone piece ahead on that line. Reach the far row for a crossing — if the opponent cannot answer with a crossing on the next turn, you win.

Your turn (White).

Select a contiguous line of your pieces, then a destination.
