Zetema

How to play. Two-player Zetema with a 65-card deck (hearts duplicated). Each turn: play a card onto its rank pile, declare a marriage (K+Q), flush, sequence, or assembly (five of a rank). Completing a five-card rank pile scores a trick. First to 300 points wins. You play against a heuristic computer.
