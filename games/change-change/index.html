How to play. Eleven coins sit in three rows (4–4–4) with one empty space. Slide an orthogonally adjacent coin into the gap. Reach a position that is symmetrical around the middle row: top and bottom must mirror each other; the unique coin and the empty space must both sit in the middle row. Play seven boards; 100 or fewer total moves is a win.
