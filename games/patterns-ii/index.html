Patterns II

How to play. One designer fills a 6×6 grid with symbols ○ + · × in an ordered pattern. Guessers query squares, then fill remaining cells (guesses in parentheses scoring). Score +1/−1 per guessed cell. Designer scores twice the gap between best and worst guesser (with give-up penalties). Here you design; two computer guessers query and score against you. Or switch to Guess mode vs a computer designer.
