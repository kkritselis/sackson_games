Domino Bead Game

How to play. You vs the computer with double-5 dominos (no blanks or sixes). Place a tile so it correctly extends two or more number-groups already on the table (groups work like crossword words — a repeating pattern locks once a number repeats, or all five faces appear). Score is the product of the lengths of groups you extend (doubled for doubles). At most three tiles may share long-side contact in a straight stack. Empty-hand or stalemate ends the game; leftover tiles cost 5 (double) or 25 points each.
