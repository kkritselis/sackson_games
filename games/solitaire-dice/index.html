Solitaire Dice

How to start: Roll five dice. Split them into two pair-sums (2–12) and one reject (1–6). You may use only three different reject numbers; once chosen, every throw must reject one of those (or take a free ride if none appear). Game ends when any reject reaches 8 marks. Combinations score only with more than 5 marks; thin combinations (1–4 marks) cost 200 each. Aim for 500+.
