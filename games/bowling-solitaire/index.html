Bowling Solitaire

How to play: Ten pin cards; three ball piles (5/3/2). Play a ball to remove 1 matching pin, or 2–3 adjacent pins whose sum’s last digit matches the ball. First ball can’t clear back row alone or center F alone. Later rolls must touch already-cleared pins. Clear all = strike/spare. Ten frames to 300.
