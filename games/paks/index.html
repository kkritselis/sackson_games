Paks

How to play. Draw from the stock. If the suit is new to the table, you must feed it and end your turn. If it matches a table suit, keep it and you may form one Pak (same-suit cards from hand totaling more than a table card). Then you may steal opposing Paks in other suits the same way. After the deck is gone, throw off Paks to match the opponent’s minimum per suit and score. First to 500 wins (this build plays hands to a running total).
