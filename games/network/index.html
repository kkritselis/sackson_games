Network

How to play. Place 10 checkers each, then relocate them. Build a network of at least 6 of your pieces connecting your two goal areas along straight orthogonal or diagonal lines (enemy pieces block). Friendly groups may not exceed 2 adjoining pieces (orthogonal or diagonal). Corners are dead. You are Black (top/bottom goals) vs the computer (White, left/right goals). Second player gets the privilege of one size-3 or size-4 group during placement.
