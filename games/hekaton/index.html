Hekaton

How to play. Cards are digits: Ace=1, 2–9 as face value, King=0. Play one card onto the table each turn, then draw. If you can arrange all table cards into numbers that add up to a multiple of 100, you score that total and clear the table. First to 5,000 points wins (shortened from 10,000 for a quicker match).
