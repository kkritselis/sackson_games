Three Musketeers

How to play. On a 5×5 board, three Musketeers face twenty-two enemies. Musketeers move orthogonally onto an enemy to capture it. Enemies move into empty orthogonal squares. Enemies win if all three Musketeers share a row or column; Musketeers win when they have no capture available on their turn.

Choose a side and begin.
