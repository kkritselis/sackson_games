Paper Boxing

How to play. Each fighter has a 4×4 board: Start in the upper-left, numbers 1–15 elsewhere. Construction is simplified: you may shuffle your board, then lock it; the computer builds its own. First player is decided by whether the sum of both lower-right numbers is odd or even. Each round both move to an unused adjacent square (including diagonal). Higher number wins the round. Winner of a round leads the next (ties: same leader). Visit all 15 numbers. A fighter who cannot move is knocked out.
