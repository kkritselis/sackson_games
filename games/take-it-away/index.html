Take It Away

How to play. Chips fill the board (blue=3, red=2, white=1). First turn: remove any white chip. After that, jump orthogonally or diagonally over an adjacent chip into an empty square; continue jumping with the same chip while possible. Collect jumped chips. At the start of your turn you may say “Take it away” and drop out. When only one player remains, that patsy keeps jumping; leftover chips count against them at 3× value. Highest score wins.
