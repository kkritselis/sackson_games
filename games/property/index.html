Property

How to play. Simplified two-player Property on an 8×8 grid. Play one red and one black number card (A–10; 9/10 are wild for any row/column). Claim empty squares, pay rent on opponent groups (doubled if a wild is used), optionally buy with purchase units (K=4, Q=2, J=1). Landing on your own square mortgages it; a second mortgage loses it. First bankruptcy ends the game — most cash wins. Vs AI.
