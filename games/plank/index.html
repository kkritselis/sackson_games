Plank

How to play. Place a plank (long sides touching) and put a matching-color marker on it, or add a marker to an existing plank. After all six markers are down, move one each turn. Win by lining up your red, white, and blue markers in a straight row — across one plank or lengthwise across three — never diagonally.
