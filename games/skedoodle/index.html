Skedoodle

How to play. Pick a master number (3–9). Players take turns writing a new whole number from 1–30 derived from the previous one by digit math, doubling, halving, squaring, or square root (from 1, jump anywhere unused). Score when your number contains the master or its digits combine to it. Highest score when no moves remain wins.
