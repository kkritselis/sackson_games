Knight Chase

How to start: Knights start in opposite near-right corners. Each turn: knight-move to an empty square, then place two markers (one on the square you left, one anywhere empty — you may not block the opponent’s only escape). After 30 markers, only moves remain; White then has 10 tries to land on Black. White wins by capture; Black wins by escaping or if anyone is immobilized.
