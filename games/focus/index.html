Focus

How to play. Stacks move orthogonally a number of spaces equal to their height (or fewer, taking only the top pieces). Control a stack by owning the top piece. Stacks cap at 5; excess from the bottom becomes captures (enemy) or reserves (yours). On your turn you may place one reserve on any square instead of moving. Immobilize the opponent (no controlled stacks and no reserves) to win. You are White vs the computer (Black).
